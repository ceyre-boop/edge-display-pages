Privacy Policy: TikTok Display Feed

Operator: TABOOST. Effective 2026-10-13.

What we collect

Only from creators who authorize the app through TikTok Login Kit: their basic profile identifier (open_id), and, via the Display API, their own public videos' id, creation time, and description text (including hashtags). We do not collect video files, images, audio, comments, followers, messages, or any data about viewers.

Why

To compute aggregate daily counts of brand mentions across a roster of creators, for internal research.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share data.
  • We do not use data for advertising or profiling.
  • We do not report on individual creators. Output is aggregate only, and a daily row is produced only when at least 10 creators were active.
  • We do not publish content to TikTok on your behalf.

Retention

Access tokens are stored on the operator's machine with restricted file permissions. Video text metadata is retained while the research is ongoing, then deleted. Tokens are deleted when they expire or when you revoke access.

Revocation and deletion

You can revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and websites. You can also ask us to delete your stored token and video metadata by emailing the contact below; we act on requests within 30 days.
